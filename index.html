How to choose a fire pump

What is a fire pump?

A fire pump raises water pressure for a sprinkler, standpipe or hydrant system when the public supply is not strong enough.

Electric or diesel?

Choose electric when power is reliable. Choose diesel when the pump must run without grid power, or when the local authority requires a second driver.

What do we need to size a pump?

Send us the required flow, the pressure needed at that flow, the water source and its pressure, and the standard you are designing to, such as NFPA 20.

How often should a fire pump be tested?

Fire pumps are run and flow-tested on a schedule set by the standard and your local authority. We can supply test documentation with each set.

Frequently asked questions

What is a fire pump?

A dedicated pump that raises water pressure for a building's sprinkler, standpipe or hydrant system.

Should I choose an electric or diesel fire pump?

Electric suits reliable power. Diesel suits sites where power may fail or a second driver is required.

What information is needed to size a fire pump?

Required flow, required pressure, water source and pressure, and the applicable standard.

Do fire pumps need regular testing?

Yes. Testing intervals are set by the installation standard and local authority, so check what applies to your site.
