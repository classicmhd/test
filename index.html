Questions

Fire pump basics

Which fire pumps are UL/FM approved?

UL and FM approval is held by specific pump models and ratings, not by a whole brand. Ask for the approval certificate of the exact model and flow rating you intend to specify.

Do I need a diesel fire pump in Bangladesh?

Where grid power is unreliable, or where the project's fire engineer requires an independent supply, a diesel engine driver is used, often alongside an electric pump. Your consultant and the authority having jurisdiction decide the final arrangement.

Which standards guide fire pump design?

NFPA 20 is the widely used international reference for stationary fire pumps. Projects in Bangladesh must also follow the Bangladesh National Building Code and the requirements of the Fire Service and Civil Defence.

What do you need to quote a fire pump set?

Required flow and pressure, water source and suction conditions, available power, building or hazard type, and the approvals your project requires.
