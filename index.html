Questions

Fire pump basics

Short answers to what specifiers and building owners ask most.

What does a fire pump do?

A fire pump raises water pressure so sprinkler, standpipe and hydrant systems deliver the required flow when the public supply or a gravity tank cannot.

Should I choose an electric or a diesel fire pump?

Electric drivers suit sites with a reliable, code-compliant power supply. Diesel drivers run independently of the grid and are chosen where power may fail or where the authority having jurisdiction requires it. Many sites install one of each.

Which standards apply to fire pump installations?

NFPA 20 is the widely referenced standard for stationary fire pump installation. Local codes and the authority having jurisdiction may add requirements, and many projects require UL or FM listed equipment.

What information is needed to select a fire pump?

The required flow and pressure, the water source and suction conditions, the building or hazard type, available power, and the approvals your project requires.
